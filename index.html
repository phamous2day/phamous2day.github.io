About Me

I'm a Lead Product Designer who's obsessed with clarity, speed, and results. At Salesforce, I led UX for Inbox and Sales Cloud—tools that helped drive $7.58B in revenue and boosted user productivity by 90%, thus significantly reducing task completion time from 10 minutes to 1 minute.

At Cox Automotive, I worked on the largest self-managed vehicle transport platform in the U.S., streamlining logistics for millions of shipments. At Mailchimp, fine-tuned my compassion for customer pain points and goals for a platform sending 1B+ emails daily to 14M+ users.

Now at CallRail, I lead design for marketing analytics tools that help small businesses punch above their weight. I bring deep UX expertise, technical fluency in the MEAN stack, and a bias toward action.

I thrive on solving hard problems with small, fast-moving teams.
