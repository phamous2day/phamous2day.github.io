About Me

I'm a Lead Product Designer with 10+ years of experience making complex tools feel effortless. I specialize in B2B products—analytics dashboards, CRM workflows, logistics platforms—where clarity directly drives revenue.

At Salesforce, I led UX for Inbox and Sales Cloud as part of a team that drove $7.58B in combined revenue impact, redesigning task flows that cut completion time from 10 minutes to 1 minute. At Cox Automotive, I redesigned core workflows on Central Dispatch, the largest self-managed vehicle transport platform in the U.S. At Mailchimp, I designed onboarding and campaign experiences for a platform serving 14M+ users and sending 1B+ emails daily.

Now at CallRail, I lead design for marketing analytics tools that help small businesses make smarter decisions from their call data. I work best on small, fast-moving teams where I can go from whiteboard to shipped product quickly.

I thrive on solving hard problems with small, fast-moving teams.
